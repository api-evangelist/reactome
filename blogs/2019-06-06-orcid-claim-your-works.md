Reactome is a curated database, it critically depends on the expertise of curators and domain experts as volunteer pathway authors and reviewers. Over 700 scientists have so far contributed to Reactome content. If you are one of them, we have great news for you: Your Reactome contribution can be included directly into your ORCID profile.
