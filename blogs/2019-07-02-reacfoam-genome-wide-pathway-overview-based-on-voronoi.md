As part of our continuous effort to provide visually attractive and more user-friendly access to our biological pathways, we are pleased to announce our new high level pathways overview visualisation based on Voronoi tessellation. Following any pathway analysis , the pathway overview provides a new icon which leads to a comprehensive, highly visual, interactive overview of pathway analysis results. The functionality is available on both desktop and mobile platforms.
