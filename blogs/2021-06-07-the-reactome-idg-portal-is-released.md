As part of the Cutting Edge Informatics Tools (CEIT) for the Illuminating the Druggable (IDG) Program, the Reactome group has built a new tool, the Reactome IDG Portal , that utilizes the Reactome knowledgebase to systematically illuminate interactions of dark proteins with other proteins and biological entities, allowing evaluation of these understudied proteins via their localizations and potential interactions, and facilitating the design of experiments to test their functions.
