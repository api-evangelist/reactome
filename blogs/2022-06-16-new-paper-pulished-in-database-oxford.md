A new research article entitled “ Evaluating the predictive accuracy of curated biological pathways in a public knowledgebase ” has been published in the Database (Oxford) journal. The paper describes the utility of Reactome pathways for predicting functional consequences of genetic perturbations. Predictions of perturbation effects based on Reactome pathways were compared against published empirical observations.
