We are excited to share that the Reactome Knowledgebase has officially received CoreTrustSeal certification, a recognition awarded to data repositories that meet high standards for trustworthiness, sustainability, and open data practices. This milestone reflects years of work by the Reactome team to ensure that our data is not only freely available and open-source, but also stable, well-documented, and responsibly maintained. CoreTrustSeal certification signals to users, funders, and collaborators that Reactome is committed to the long-term availability and stewardship of its content.
